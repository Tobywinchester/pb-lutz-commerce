# PerfectlyBasics + Lutz — commerce options

An interactive, merchant-facing comparison of four Shopify configurations.

- Monthly and yearly platform, POS and payment-cost breakdowns.
- Editable planning forecasts and billing assumptions.
- Native Plus POS processing at the quoted 0.8%; Advanced native POS at 1.1%.
- External retail in option 2 has EUR0 Shopify retail processing; the external provider’s own charges remain separate.
- Shopify Payments online is modelled in all four configurations.
- iDEAL rates and the supplied payment mix are included, not added as separate savings.
- Public conversion benchmarks and Plus implementation stories are context, not guaranteed outcomes.

These are merchant-provided planning forecasts and proposed commercial assumptions, not independently verified current revenue or a binding offer. Contract definitions and final written terms govern. Implementation, retained systems, fee taxes and other exclusions are explained in the page.

The site runs entirely in the browser and does not transmit calculator inputs. A robots noindex directive is included, but this is a PUBLIC repository and website, not access-controlled or confidential hosting.

Brand names, logos and linked campaign/case-study imagery remain the property of their respective owners; attribution and public source links are provided in the page. This is a commercial discussion document, not an endorsement or an isolated Advanced-to-Plus performance experiment.
