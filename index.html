02 / WHERE PLUS CAN EARN ITS PLACE

More control.
Where Lutz needs it.

At Lutz’s online-and-retail scale, the case is operational and commercial—not revenue alone. These are specific Plus differences to validate against the proposed build, not a generic enterprise feature list.

IMPORTANT BASELINE

Advanced is already capable.

Shopify Checkout, Shopify Payments, Shop Pay, core Shopify Flow automations and professional POS workflows are not Plus-only. Do not attribute their whole benefit to an Advanced-to-Plus upgrade. Plus needs to solve a more specific checkout, custom-logic, capacity or operating requirement.

03 / UPGRADE EVIDENCE, NOT A GUARANTEE

Reported results.
The context matters.

Published Plus upgrade projects report conversion, merchandising and operating outcomes. Some levers are Plus-specific; others come from wider rebuilds. Each case labels the actual history and limits—not a percentage to paste into Lutz’s forecast.

Keep the comparison honest. Generic Shopify-versus-other-platform and Shop Pay-versus-guest benchmarks are not measured Advanced-to-Plus uplifts. They are not used as incremental revenue in this business case. Source classifications below distinguish a verified Advanced upgrade from a broader Shopify-to-Plus implementation.
01

Find the actual checkout gap.

Name the pre-payment requirement, promotion/loyalty rule or product-merchandising limitation. Compare the Advanced app/build alternative and agree the success measure.

02

Measure the operating load.

Have the implementation partner quantify API peaks, campaign setup work, manual exceptions and support responsibilities. Capacity headroom is not automatically time or cost saved.

03

Make value accountable.

Test one meaningful Plus-specific change against the existing baseline. Use incremental contribution, hours removed or a documented requirement—not total sales as profit.
