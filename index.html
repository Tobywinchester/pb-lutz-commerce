Payment rates and calculation basis

Expected online transaction counts use the historic AOV supplied for each brand. Payment mix is assumed to apply to both value and counts; method-specific AOV is not available. Base cards are domestic/EEA; actual non-EEA/Amex mix may change fees. PayPal uses a published-standard estimate, not a verified negotiated merchant rate.

Retail processing is the retail forecast × card-payment share × the applicable rate: 0.8% for Plus in options 1/3, 1.1% for Advanced in option 4. Neither Shopify rate is applied to external option 2.

Platform terms, billing and what is excluded

The proposed both-Plus combined minimum is €3,600/month. Lutz standalone Plus uses a €2,100/month minimum. The model uses a three-year Plus term. Modelled variable platform fees combine 0.35% of online GMV and 0.25% of recorded Lutz retail GMV for the Plus shop(s). Each billing month is charged at the greater of that combined variable amount or the applicable Plus minimum—not the minimum plus the variable amount. Final contract definitions govern.

Advanced is €289/brand/month equivalent when annually billed, or €384 month-to-month. Paid POS Pro is €79/location/month equivalent on annual billing, or €89 month-to-month. Options 2/4 include two Advanced stores; option 3 includes one. Option 4 includes two paid POS Pro locations; the two Lutz locations are included with Plus in options 1/3.

Hardware, migration, agency/build work, ERP, apps/connectors, loyalty/returns integration, support retainers, fee taxes, chargeback/FX extras and unconfirmed incentives are excluded—not zero. Option 2 uses Shopify Payments online and an external provider only for retail. Shopify retail processing and the Shopify third-party retail transaction fee are €0 in that route, on any plan. The external provider’s own POS and acquiring charges remain separate. The Advanced 0.6% Shopify third-party online transaction fee applies only if an external processor is used online—not simply because retail POS is external. Shopify Payments online processing fees remain included.
