## Scope and assumptions

Two routes only: Lutz Plus / PerfectlyBasics Advanced, or both Advanced with Shopify POS for Lutz's two retail locations. PB stays Advanced in both. Current annual planning volumes: Lutz retail EUR4m, Lutz online EUR2.4m, PB online EUR2.4m. Existing platform/POS/online fee assumptions remain itemised.

The starting retail illustration is90%debit/10%credit/0%Amex by captured card-sales VALUE, not a verified merchant mix. The default card-capture share100% and historical all-retail AOV proxy are assumptions. Mix, capture and AOV or entered annual card-paid orders are editable. The fixed order fee is not allocated using sales-value shares, and entered card-paid orders are not capture-scaled a second time.

A EUR6300 TOTAL one-off first-year credit applies across the first three monthly Lutz Plus SUBSCRIPTION bills only. It does not reduce PB Advanced, POS or payment-processing fees; ongoing costs do not repeat it. The offered lifetime Rebuy/AfterSell access covers checkout-customisation features subject to supplier scope/terms/setup confirmation. No app value is deducted again from TCO.

## Interpretation and implementation

The review-draft/illustrative labels are intentional: this is not a binding all-in quote, verified retail mix or a guaranteed ROI forecast. No automatic conversion uplift or incumbent-payment saving is claimed. Hardware, migration/build, agency work, ERP/apps/connectors/support, taxes and other disclosed extras are excluded, not priced at zero.

Plus capability and merchant implementation stories retain their Advanced baselines, actual prior-plan classifications and wider-project confounders. Reported case outcomes are not a forecast for Lutz or attributed specifically to Rebuy/AfterSell.

The page is self-contained. Calculator changes stay in the browser page. Monthly figures are annual equivalents, not promised monthly invoices. Saved HTML and CSV preserve the chosen assumptions.
